# Privacy Policy for Market in 30
Last updated: October 30, 2026

Market in 30 publishes short news videos to Instagram, Facebook and YouTube from accounts owned by its creator. It uses Google, Facebook and Instagram login only so the creator can upload their own videos to their own accounts.

We do not collect, sell or share personal data from viewers or visitors. The app stores only the access keys needed to upload the creator's own videos, and keeps them private on the creator's own computer.
